# Changelog

## 0.1.2
- New: **mod-menu-only package** (`ui-plus-modmenu-only-0.1.2.zip`): same DLL, only the mod list screen is on.
- Fixed: a worn saddle couldn't be removed from a dino's inventory. It now unequips (ARK drops it at your feet).
- New: using a saddle from your inventory while a dino's inventory is open equips it on the dino, swapping out a worn one (like vanilla).
- Fixed: LT+X / RT+X "all of this type" only worked player to dino. Now RT+X always sends to the dino/box and LT+X always takes to you, from either side.
- Fixed: cooking stations (campfire, cooking pot, forge...) now show their item slots next to the fuel.
- Hints: the dino panel shows "Hold: Take all" and your panel "Hold: Send all" on Y.
- Both zips now include LICENSE.txt.

## 0.1.1
- Fixed: the Save game button (Options tab) said "no world loaded" in a single-player world.
- Fixed: the map's right-side list couldn't be navigated past the 2nd entry when entries share a name (beds, dinos). The mouse wheel now scrolls that list.
- Removed: lunchbox auto-eat (it changed gameplay).

## 0.1.0
- First community release: controller-first inventory, crafting, station, container and map screens, radial wheels, new HUD, gamepad layout and mod list screen.
